# Interview Synthesis One-Pager

**Fill this in after interview 5** (or update it rolling, after each). Purpose: force *counting* over *vibing.* Your brain will want to remember the enthusiastic interviews and forget the lukewarm ones. This sheet stops that.

**The discipline:** every cell below is a number or a verbatim quote. No adjectives, no "seemed pretty interested." If you can't put a number or a real quote in a cell, you don't have the data — which is itself a finding.

## The Strongest Quote

## Pivot Signal

Did a *different* problem surface repeatedly — one you weren't asking about? (Cash flow timing, getting paid faster, finding good crew, estimating, something else.) If the same non-hypothesis pain came up in 3+ interviews, that's louder than anything.
